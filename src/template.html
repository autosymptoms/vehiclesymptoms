Common questions

What's wrong with my car?

Start by describing what you notice: a noise, a smell, a warning light, shaking, or a leak, and when it happens. Our symptom guides ask a few quick questions and narrow it down to the most likely causes, with an urgency rating so you know whether it's safe to keep driving.

Is it safe to drive my car?

Every result starts with an urgency rating: stop now, get it checked within a few days, book a check when convenient, or safe to check yourself first. If a red warning light is flashing, you smell fuel or burning, or the brakes or steering feel wrong, pull over safely and get help.

How much will the repair cost?

Use the price checker to prepare an itemized estimate request for parts, labor, fees and the total. For selected vehicles and repairs, it also explains which services to compare on your quote.

Does this replace a mechanic?

No. It helps you understand the problem and how urgent it is, and gives you a summary to take to a mechanic. A professional inspection is the only way to confirm a diagnosis.

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up and no fees. If you'd like a copy of your result, you can email it to yourself; we don't send marketing email.
